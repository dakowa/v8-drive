V8 Drive versions

Every release stays playable. Older releases keep their own settings and drives, so each one sounds exactly as it shipped. There is no v6.

v11 currentOpen ›
  • Steady rpm (cruising, motorway) no longer pulses: the short engine loops used to repeat exactly every ~0.75 s. The recorded engine cycles now play in an ever-changing order, and a steady rpm plays one recording instead of a blend of two.
  • Cruising: small jitter in the throttle estimate (road, phone mount) is averaged out so the sound doesn't wobble between on- and off-throttle recordings; revs glide over the 1 Hz GPS speed corrections. Pressing or lifting still responds at once; launches are unchanged.
  • v10 verdict: idle, acceleration and deceleration really good; steady rpm awful (oscillating, didn't sound recorded).
v10Open ›
  • Gentle pull-away: the revs ease up from idle with the pedal instead of jumping from about 1000 to 1500 rpm in a tenth of a second. A firm or full throttle launches as before.
  • Low-rpm sound test (Settings, "Low-rpm sound (test)"), four different takes on 1100–2100 rpm, everything above unchanged: A the GT500 as in v9 (default), B a Ford Galaxie V8 held at steady revs 1200–1840 rpm, C a Chevy Caprice V8 at steady revs 1150–1550 rpm, D the GT500's clean 2100 rpm sound slowed down below that. B and C are different engines, tone-matched to the GT500. Temporary: the losers go once you pick one.
  • Upshift rpm adjustable per tier (gentle, medium, firm, max), 1500–6000 rpm, next to each g slider; moving one keeps the others in order. Above 5000 rpm the sound rises more slowly (the recordings end near 4400), so a 6000 shift sounds like about 5500.
  • Only real drives are recorded: nothing while the simulator is on.
  • The "Sounded wrong / great" buttons are gone; say what sounded off instead.
v9Open ›
  • One engine pitch slider again (the v5 "Pitch test" A/B is gone). Nothing plays faster than at pitch 1.10: the idle stays at most 1.10× its recorded speed, and above 1.10 the top end flattens out near 4300 rpm, where the GT500 recordings end, instead of running the loops up to 1.8× too fast.
  • 5–15 km/h: crawling on a light throttle plays the real idle (the clutch slips just above idle); a firm throttle or 16 km/h hands over to the engine. Idle and engine switch with hysteresis and a short crossfade, never a lingering blend of a sped-up idle and the engine. Pulling away slips at 1500 rpm or more. The low-speed A/B toggle is gone.
  • The 1100–2100 rpm loops are re-picked for the least RPM drift within each loop; 2350 rpm and up are unchanged.
  • Idle picker back, as in v3: I3 Chrysler (default) or I1 Mustang 5.0.
  • Version control: release tags, this changelog, every earlier release playable at /v/N/ with its own settings and drives, listed at /v/ (version badge in the header).
  • A second, fully recorded engine was looked for and not added: the best candidate (a Ford Galaxie V8 with steady free-revs) has gaps of 18–38% between its recorded speeds and no on-throttle sound, so it would have needed stretching or stitching.
