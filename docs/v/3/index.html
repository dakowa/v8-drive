  About & credits
  

    
Sound engine. Constant-RPM loops cut from the recordings every 200–300 rpm, on- and off-throttle. They're phase-aligned to the engine cycle and crossfaded by RPM and throttle. At idle, a long real idle recording plays untouched.

    
Recordings: Ford Mustang GT500 by VacekH on Freesound (CC0). RPM was measured from the firing-order harmonics.

    
Idle recordings (Freesound): 1964 Chrysler Windsor by lonemonk (CC BY 4.0); 1985 Ford Mustang 5.0 by lmbubec (CC0).

    
Gearing: S550 Mustang GT. 10R80 ratios from Ford's chart, MT82 manual ratios from CJ Pony Parts, 255/40R19 tyres.

    
Keep the screen on with the app open. iOS stops web audio and sensors when the screen locks.
